🃏 Controls Reference:
• Touch / Mouse: Tap bottom bar buttons, or tap felt table after hand to deal again.
• Action Hotkeys: H Hit  •  S Stand  •  D Double / Deal  •  P Split  •  B Bet  •  Enter / Space Confirm.
• Wager Adjustment: ↑ / ↓ or + / - (±$10)  •  1 / 2 (±$50)  •  X Max Bet  •  C Min Bet ($10).
• Trackball & Arrows: Roll Left/Right to highlight action buttons, Roll Up/Down to Bet/Hit/Stand.
